About

I'm Royal. I build AI systems alone, from scratch, in Phnom Penh.

I didn't come from a big tech company. I didn't raise money. I don't have a team. What I have is a laptop and a system I've built over months of daily work.

What I care about

  • Local-first AI — Systems that run on your own hardware, no cloud dependency, no per-message fees.
  • Long-term memory — AI that remembers across sessions, because that's where every enterprise AI project fails.
  • Cryptographic trust — Every action the system takes is recorded in a hash-chained audit trail that cannot be altered.
  • Physical sensing — Reading gaze, voice, and presence through ordinary hardware.
  • Containment — AI that cannot escape its own permission boundaries, no matter what.

I'm interested in connecting with other builders — especially people working on local inference, autonomous agents, computer use, and AIOS architecture.
